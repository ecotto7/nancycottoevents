Inquiries & Clarifications

About NCE: Meet the force behind Nancy Cotto Events – I’m Nancy, residing in the captivating St. Pete with my husband and our aussome pup, Tiki. With 25 years immersed in the wedding realm, I’ve transitioned from Catering Director to Venue Manager and, post-2020, embraced my true calling as a girl-boss planner. My essence is a blend of fun and friendliness, fueled by an unmatched passion for weddings. Honest, down-to-earth, and creatively driven, I’ve orchestrated over 2000 weddings, perfecting the craft with an organized, get-it-done attitude. Let’s create a fabulous experience for your special day, relieving you of all stress.

Why Choose a Wedding Coordinator: In the intricate tapestry of wedding planning, I offer a guiding hand, saving you from the 250-hour average planning endeavor. Beyond time-saving, a coordinator prioritizes vendors, streamlines budgets, provides qualified referrals, attends vendor meetings, reviews contracts, negotiates on your behalf, and sparks creative decor and design ideas. As your wedding approaches, I confirm vendors, create timelines, run rehearsals, ensuring a stress-free day for you, your family, and friends.

Our Locations: Based in the mesmerizing St. Petersburg, including Sarasota and Tampa Bay, we also extend our magic virtually and to destination weddings. Share your dreams, and let’s embark on planning your ultimate wedding.
